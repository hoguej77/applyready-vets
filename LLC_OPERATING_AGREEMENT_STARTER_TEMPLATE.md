# LLC operating agreement starter template

This is a starter template for attorney review. Do not treat it as legal advice or a final operating agreement.

## Limited Liability Company Operating Agreement of [LEGAL COMPANY NAME], LLC

Effective date: [DATE]

State of formation: California

## 1. Company

The company name is [LEGAL COMPANY NAME], LLC.

The company was formed by filing Articles of Organization with the California Secretary of State.

The company's principal business is software development, mobile applications, career-document tools, AI-assisted writing workflows, and related services.

## 2. Member

The initial member is:

| Member | Ownership percentage | Initial contribution |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| [FOUNDER LEGAL NAME] | 100% | [CASH/IP/SERVICES DESCRIPTION] |

## 3. Management

The company is manager-managed / member-managed: [CHOOSE ONE].

Initial manager: [NAME].

The manager may operate the business, open bank accounts, sign contracts, hire contractors, manage App Store accounts, manage intellectual property, and take actions reasonably necessary to run the company.

## 4. Intellectual property

All software, source code, documentation, names, logos, product designs, prompts, workflows, business plans, and related intellectual property developed for ApplyReady Vets or any successor product should be assigned to the company through a separate written IP assignment.

Any founder-created pre-formation assets contributed to the company should be listed in an attached schedule.

## 5. Bank accounts and records

Company funds must be kept separate from personal funds.

The company should maintain records for:

- formation filings;
- EIN confirmation;
- tax payments;
- App Store agreements;
- contracts;
- invoices;
- receipts;
- software licenses;
- privacy and security policies;
- member decisions.

## 6. Taxes

The company will be treated as a disregarded entity unless another tax election is made.

The company must pay required California taxes and file required federal, state, and local returns.

The member should consult a CPA before making any S-corporation or corporate tax election.

## 7. Distributions

Distributions may be made only after setting aside reasonable reserves for taxes, App Store costs, hosting, legal, accounting, refunds, contractors, and operating expenses.

## 8. Liability and indemnification

The company should indemnify the member and manager to the fullest extent allowed by law for actions taken in good faith on behalf of the company.

This section requires attorney review.

## 9. Transfers

No ownership interest may be transferred without written approval and updated company records.

## 10. Dissolution

The company may be dissolved by written decision of the member or as required by law.

Before dissolution, the company should settle debts, preserve records, cancel state registrations, close tax accounts, and handle user data according to published policies.

## 11. Amendments

This agreement may be amended only in writing.
