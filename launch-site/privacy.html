What you choose to provide

Do not provide information that is classified, controlled, medically sensitive, unnecessary for a job application, or prohibited by your employer or service obligations.

Local processing and storage

The private career engine, readiness analysis, Truth Lock review, LinkedIn copy kit, job tracker, and PDF/RTF/TXT export run locally. App-managed workspaces and recovery copies use iOS file protection. A development-service token, if one is configured in a development build, is stored in iOS Keychain.

User-authored files stored in Application Support or Documents may be included in Apple system backups according to the device owner's Apple settings. We do not receive or control those backups.

Apple on-device assistance

If you enable AI and provide explicit consent on an eligible device, Apple's on-device Foundation Models may propose bounded wording based on a limited evidence subset. Availability depends on the device, language, region, settings, and operating system. Proposals are treated as untrusted and must pass local checks and your review.

Optional service status

The reviewed public Release configuration has no default ApplyReady Vets cloud-service endpoint. Therefore, installing or using this release does not automatically send your resume, job posting, or profile to [FINAL LEGAL ENTITY] or OpenAI. A future optional service must not be enabled until this policy names its provider, hosting region, purposes, content sent, retention, deletion, security, subprocessors, and consent controls, and the App Store privacy answers are updated.

Sharing, selling, advertising, and tracking

The reviewed app does not sell personal information, run advertising, track users across other companies' apps or websites, or include a third-party analytics SDK. We do not sell individual resume content or veteran activity to employers. Apple processes App Store purchases under Apple's terms and privacy practices.

External links and exported copies

The app can open external job and official-resource sites and can share files using Apple's system interfaces. Those destinations receive information only when you choose to visit or share. Their privacy practices apply after you leave the app. We do not collect LinkedIn, Indeed, Workday, USAJOBS, or employer passwords.

Retention and deletion

App-managed local information remains until you delete an application, erase local app data, uninstall the app, or the operating system removes it. Use Settings → Privacy controls → Erase local app data for the current installation. You must separately delete exported/shared copies and manage Apple system backups through Apple settings.

The current reviewed release has no public account database. If accounts or server retention are introduced, the app and this policy must provide the required account-deletion and retention details before release.

Security

Controls include iOS sandboxing and file protection, Keychain storage for tokens, protected atomic writes, integrity checks and recovery copies, bounded imports, privacy/OPSEC warnings, safe-link rules, explicit consent, and final export validation.
