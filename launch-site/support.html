Start safely

  1. Use the built-in safe demo before importing personal material.
  2. Import only the resume and job information needed for the application.
  3. Review every privacy/OPSEC warning and every Truth Lock proposal.
  4. Open exported files in Files and inspect the PDF, RTF, and TXT versions before sending them.

Files and exports

Use the app's Browse Files action to open its Files-visible Output folder. Employer-facing exports are separate from the private review report. Once you copy or share a file outside the app, the receiving app or person controls that copy.

Purchase and restore

One completed workspace is free. ApplyReady Vets Complete is the only consumer purchase and unlocks unlimited tracked workspaces. Apple displays the local price and processes payment.

  • If a purchase is missing, open Complete & pricing and choose Restore purchases while signed into the Apple Account that bought it.
  • For refund eligibility or a refund request, use Apple's Report a Problem service. [FINAL LEGAL ENTITY] cannot promise or deny an Apple refund.
  • Never pay anyone by Zelle, text message, wire, gift card, or cryptocurrency to unlock the app.

Delete local app data

Use Settings → Privacy controls → Erase local app data. This removes app-managed workspaces, recovery and quarantine copies, tracker state, tokens, managed exports, shared inbox data, widget data, and Spotlight records on the current installation. Copies you exported or shared must be deleted where you placed them. Apple system backups are managed through the device owner's Apple settings and are not controlled by ApplyReady Vets.

AI and assistance

The deterministic private core works without generative AI. Eligible Apple on-device assistance requires your explicit AI consent and device/runtime availability. This release does not configure a public ApplyReady Vets cloud-service endpoint. AI can still be incomplete or wrong; approve only statements supported by your experience.

Safety and urgent help

ApplyReady Vets is not an emergency, crisis, legal, benefits, medical, or accredited claims service. For immediate danger call local emergency services. For U.S. veteran crisis support, use the official Veterans Crisis Line.
